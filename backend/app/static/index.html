Functions

Ask a question, translate, draft Cantonese script, or practise HKDSE Math with the saved text model. Image and storyboard uses the OpenRouter Images API; those jobs are not sent as chat completions. Video stays off this form.

Document upload

Accepted: .pdf, .docx, .doc, .txt, .md, .xlsx, .xls, .csv, .json. Maximum 10 MB. .doc and .xls are stored, but text extraction needs a converted copy. Chat for RAG uses the extracted text as context.

    Chat

    Uses the saved text model. Image and video functions are not sent as chat completions.

    Mode

    Photo of a printed question. Jpeg, png, webp, or heic. Maximum 10 MB. This is not a syllabus upload.

    Image and storyboard

    Uses the saved image model. Image jobs are not sent as chat completions. The unit price above is recorded, not a live quote.

    Guided prompt
    Person

    Fill this when the subject is a person. Empty fields are left out of the assembled prompt.

    Output

    1 to 10 photos. Extra photos vary facial expression (neutral, slight smile, smile, focused, speaking, surprised) and movement (idle, walking, turning, reaching, sitting, stepping). Recorded price is shown times the photo count.

    Reference photos

    Up to 5 jpeg, png, or webp files. 10 MB each. Used as OpenRouter input_references.
